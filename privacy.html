Deen Files

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Deen Files is a client for your own Telegram account. It does not run a server and does not receive your files. Everything you store is kept in a private Telegram channel that belongs to you.

1. Who we are

Deen Files ("the app", "we") is an Android application by Deenmate that lets you keep files in a private Telegram channel and browse them like a cloud drive. The app is distributed through Google Play and other app stores.

2. What the app handles

  • Telegram login data. Your phone number and login code are sent directly to Telegram to sign in. The app does not send them anywhere else.
  • Your files. Photos, videos, documents and folders you choose to upload are sent to Telegram and stored in your private channel. If you turn on encryption, file contents, names, captions and the folder tree are encrypted on your device before they leave it.
  • App data on your device. A local index, settings, thumbnails and caches are stored on your phone. The Telegram session and local database are protected with keys held by Android Keystore.
  • No advertising or analytics. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracking.

3. Where your data goes

Your files and account data go to Telegram, the storage provider you chose. Telegram's own privacy policy applies to data stored on its servers: telegram.org/privacy. The developer of Deen Files has no server, no database of your files and no way to read your channel or your encryption keys.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos and videos — to back up the media you select.
  • All files access — optional, and only if you enable automatic backup of folders such as Downloads or Documents. Without it you can still upload files manually.
  • Notifications — to show transfer and backup progress.
  • Ignore battery optimisation — optional; keeps long backups running.
  • Biometric / device credential — optional app lock.

You can deny any optional permission and still use the app.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The only third party involved is Telegram, which stores the files you upload. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers or data brokers in the app.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Delete a file in the app to remove it from your channel (and from Telegram, subject to Telegram's retention rules).
  • Delete the drive or the private channel in Telegram to remove everything stored there.
  • Log out or uninstall the app to remove the local session, index, thumbnails and caches from your phone.
  • Encryption keys live on your device. If you lose them, the encrypted data cannot be recovered — this is intentional.

7. Children

Deen Files is not directed to children under 13. You must have a Telegram account and be old enough to use Telegram under its terms.

8. Security

The app uses Android Keystore to protect the local database and Telegram session. Optional AES-256-GCM encryption protects file bodies, names, captions and the folder tree before upload. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device lock and any exported encryption key safe.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
