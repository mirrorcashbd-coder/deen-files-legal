Deen Files

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Deen Files is a client for your own Telegram account. It does not run a server and does not receive your files. Everything you store is kept in a private Telegram channel that belongs to you.

1. Who we are

Deen Files ("the app", "we") is an Android file manager and cloud drive by Deenmate. It lets you browse your phone storage and back up your files to a private Telegram channel or to cloud accounts you configure yourself. The app is distributed on Google Play and other stores. The developer is Deenmate ("we"), the contact address is shown at the bottom of this page.

2. What the app handles

  • Telegram login data. Your phone number and login code are sent directly to Telegram over an encrypted connection to sign in. The app does not send your phone number, code or session to us or to any other party.
  • Your files. Photos, videos, documents, app packages, archives and whole folders you choose to back up are stored in the private Telegram channel you select, or in a cloud account you configure yourself (Cloudflare R2, S3, FTP, WebDAV or Google Drive). If you enable encryption, file contents, names, captions and the folder tree are encrypted on your device before they leave it.
  • Local data on your device. The app keeps an index of your files, thumbnails, cache, your settings and session in an on-device database. The Telegram session and database are protected with keys held by Android Keystore.
  • No accounts with us, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app has no Deenmate account system, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracking SDK. We never receive your files and can never read them.

3. Where your data goes

Your files and account data go only to the storage you point the app at:

  • Telegram — the files you back up live in your own private channel. Telegram's privacy policy applies: telegram.org/privacy.
  • Cloud accounts you configure yourself — Cloudflare R2, Amazon S3, FTP, WebDAV or Google Drive receive the files you upload to them. Each provider's terms and privacy policy applies. We do not receive them.
  • Android OS storage — the app reads/writes files on your device only to show them, copy/move/rename them, and to restore or back up what you asked for.

The developer of Deen Files runs no server that holds your files, no database of your files, and has no way to read your channel or your cloud account data.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos and videos — to back up media you select.
  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — optional, and only if you enable automatic backup of whole folders such as Downloads or Documents. Without it you can still upload files manually. You can revoke it at any time in system settings and the app still works for manual uploads.
  • Notifications — to show transfer and backup progress.
  • Biometric / device credential — optional app lock.
  • Boot completed — so backups can resume after a reboot when you enabled scheduled backup.
  • Foreground service (data sync) — to keep long uploads/downloads running with an ongoing notification.

You can deny any optional permission and still use the app.

5. What we do not collect and do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we never see it.
  • We do not create accounts or profiles on our servers — there is no server.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, fingerprinting, crash reporting that contains file data, or advertising networks.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera or messages — none of those permissions are used.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Delete a file in the app to remove it from your Telegram channel or cloud account (subject to that provider's retention rules).
  • Delete a drive or channel inside Telegram to remove everything stored there.
  • Sign out and delete the drive from within the app, or uninstall the app, to remove the local session, index, thumbnails and caches from your phone.
  • Encryption keys live on your device. If you lose them, encrypted data cannot be recovered — this is intentional.

7. Your rights

Because nothing is stored on developer servers, all your data stays under your control. You may access, export, delete or stop sharing any of it at any time, using the app controls or the storage provider's own settings. For anything else, contact us through the address below and we will help or point you to the right place.

8. Children

Deen Files is not directed to children under 13. You must have a Telegram account (or a cloud account) and be old enough under the terms of that provider to use this app.

9. Security

The app uses Android Keystore to protect the Telegram session and the on-device database. Optional AES-256-GCM encryption protects file bodies, names and the folder tree before upload. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device lock and any exported encryption key safe.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact
